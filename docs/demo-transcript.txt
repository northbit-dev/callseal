
CallSeal — prove your trading track record, never leak a call.
Every step below runs the compiled Compact circuits (compact-runtime). A failed assert = no valid proof.

oracle   deploys CallSeal: min move 2.00%, proof window 1 round
oracle   round 0 closes: BTC 98000  ETH 3500  ADA 0.5  NIGHT 0.05
alice    registers as analyst  pk=5a7fa8fa…
bob      registers as analyst  pk=e9a22bd1…

2) Gaming attempts are rejected inside the proof.
bob      tries "BTC >= 98500" (0.5% away)  → rejected: call too close to the last close

3) Market moves. Oracle publishes closes.
oracle   round 1 closes: BTC 99200  ETH 3460  ADA 0.505  NIGHT 0.051
oracle   round 2 closes: BTC 102300  ETH 3420  ADA 0.531  NIGHT 0.049   (expiry round)

4) Prove hits in zero knowledge. The call itself stays sealed.
alice    proveHit #0 → HIT proven  (public learns: hit. not asset, side or level)
alice    proveHit #1 → cannot prove (call did not hit); stays sealed
bob      proveHit #2 → HIT proven  (public learns: hit. not asset, side or level)
bob      proveHit #3 → cannot prove (call did not hit); stays sealed
bob      tries to claim alice's #0 → rejected: not your call

5) Proof window closes. Hidden losers are counted, by anyone.
anyone   expireCall #1 → MISS
anyone   expireCall #3 → MISS

6) Optional: alice reveals her winner to market herself.
alice    revealCall #0 → public now: BTC >= 101000
         bob's calls stay private forever; his record is still complete.

7) Subscriber check (off-chain): alice DMs a paying subscriber the opening of #1.
  real opening matches commitment: yes    edited level matches: no
